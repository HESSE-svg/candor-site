Our plan

One workspace. Every profession that has to prove its work.

Candor began with law firms. But the machinery underneath — screen before you send, log every use, verify, sign off, and keep the record — was never specific to lawyers. It fits any regulated profession that answers to someone for how it works — and it travels across borders the same way. The road runs two ways: new professions, and new countries.

Across borders

The same plan, in more places.

The engine is jurisdiction-agnostic; the law is local. So Candor travels the way it grows across fields — one place at a time, each earned rather than claimed. Live in the U.S. today, with Dubai and the EU on the road.

How to read it

Same toolkit. Each field's own rules.

Every profession on the road gets the identical six tools. What changes underneath is what each one is tuned to — the details it screens for, the words of the attestation, the report a regulator or carrier actually asks for, and the model policy staff acknowledge. Insurance flags a claim number where law flags a docket; a financial adviser attests to suitability where an attorney attests to citations. The workspace is one; the configuration is per field.

Accounting & healthcare

Accounting and tax under their own standards; healthcare last, because handling patient data raises the bar on the whole product first.

Each field's version is configured and reviewed against that field's own rules before it ships — Candor is software, not a substitute for a professional's judgment about which rules apply to them.
