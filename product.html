Product

From first prompt to the report a carrier asks for.

Six steps, designed to sit inside how a firm already works — no browser babysitting, no intercepting anyone's traffic, no new habit to enforce.

01

Confidentiality pre-check

Before text goes to an outside AI tool, Candor scans it for client-identifying details — names, SSNs, account and policy numbers, dates, addresses, medical terms — and returns a redacted version to paste instead. Screening happens in the browser; the text itself is never stored. Try it below.

02

AI use log

Each use is recorded against the matter: which tool, what task, who ran it, when. The firm keeps an approved-tools list, and anything logged outside it is flagged — quiet visibility into what's actually being used.

03

Verification

For research and drafting, Candor pulls out case citations and surfaces hedging language worth a second look, then checks each citation's existence against a public legal database. It's a prompt for the reviewer, not a verdict — Candor never claims a citation is correct.

04

Attorney review & sign-off

A supervising attorney marks the work reviewed and attests they independently checked the citations and assertions. Entries are append-only: a change is a new record referencing the last, so the history stays intact.

05

Reports

A firm-wide summary and a renewal questionnaire mapped to the questions carriers ask — generated from the record and exportable to PDF. This is the artifact you hand across the table.

06

Policy management

Adopt a written AI-use policy; staff acknowledge it with a timestamp and re-acknowledge whenever it changes. The acknowledgements live in the same record as everything else.
