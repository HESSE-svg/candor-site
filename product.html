01

Confidentiality pre-check See it

Before text goes to an outside AI tool, Candor scans it for client identifying details: names, SSNs, account and policy numbers, dates, addresses, medical terms. It returns a redacted version to paste instead. Screening happens in the browser; the text itself is never stored. Try it below.

Confidentiality pre-check
Draft a demand for [NAME], [SSN], policy [POLICY#], re the [DATE] collision.
NAME 1SSN 1POLICY# 1DATE 1
Copy safe version

The scan runs on your device and reads names in their legal role context, not just against a word list, so it flags the client without redacting the caselaw. You paste the safe version; the original never leaves the machine.

02

AI use log See it

Each use is recorded against the matter: which tool, what task, who ran it, when. The firm keeps an approved tools list, and anything logged outside it is flagged: quiet visibility into what's actually being used.

AI use log entry
MatterRivera, J.
ToolChatGPT on approved list
TaskDrafting
Run byD. Alvarez, paralegal

A tool that is not on the approved list is flagged the moment it is logged, so a partner can see at a glance whether staff are using sanctioned tools, without watching over anyone's shoulder.

03

Verification See it

For research and drafting, Candor pulls out case citations and surfaces hedging language worth a second look, then checks each citation's existence against a public legal database. It's a prompt for the reviewer, not a verdict. Candor never claims a citation is correct.

Citation check
✓ 576 U.S. 644 Obergefell v. Hodges
⚠ 812 F.3d 4471 not found, verify manually
appearslikelyit seems

Only the bare citation is sent out for the existence check, never the client's text. The hedging words are the AI's own signals of uncertainty, surfaced so the reviewer knows where to look hardest.

04

Attorney review & sign off See it

A supervising attorney marks the work reviewed and attests they independently checked the citations and assertions. Entries are append only: a change is a new record referencing the last, so the history stays intact.

Attorney sign off
☑ I have independently verified all citations and factual assertions in this output.
M. Okonkwo, Esq. · 14 Apr 2026, 09:41 EDT

The attestation wording is fixed and deliberate. Because entries are append only, a later correction shows up as its own record, so the trail reads like a ledger, not a document someone could quietly rewrite.

05

Reports See it

Candor generates a firm wide summary, and a renewal questionnaire mapped to the questions carriers ask, both built from the record and exportable to PDF. This is the artifact you hand across the table.

Works with your case management

It files against the matters you already have.

Connect the system your firm already runs on, and AI use is recorded against real matters instead of names somebody retypes. Candor supports Clio, MyCase, Filevine, PracticePanther, and Smokeball.

Your administrator authorises it

Sign in on the platform's own site, the standard way. Candor never sees a password, and your firm can cut off access from your side at any time without asking us.

Matters are read, never kept

Candor reads the matter list when it needs it and lets it go. Your client list is not copied into our storage, and every time it is read the access log records it.

Facts without the client name

Pull the matter number, practice area, and status straight into a prompt so nobody retypes them. The matter title stays out by default, because titles usually carry the client's name.

One thing to check on your side: private integrations need a mid tier plan or above at most platforms, and the person connecting has to be an administrator.

Readiness score

Client consent tracking

Exception log

Supervision queue

Billing note

Mark whether an AI use affects what the client is billed, and note how the work was produced, at the time it happens rather than months later when nobody remembers.

Time on task, measured

Candor counts the time actually spent working with the AI and drafts a billing entry from it. What the same work would have taken a person is your estimate, never ours, and where you do not give one no comparison is recorded.

Common questions

Questions firms ask about Candor

Does Candor store what I type into an AI tool?

No. The confidentiality screen runs in your browser, and Candor keeps only the fact that a check ran and which categories fired, never the text. Retaining any underlying content is an explicit choice a firm turns on, off by default.

Whose AI does Candor use, mine or its own?

Your firm's own model, on your own key. Every prompt is screened before it reaches the model, and Candor keeps the fact of each use, not its content.

Does Candor write anything into my case-management system?

No. When you connect Clio, MyCase, or another system, Candor reads the matter list read-only when it needs it, and never writes back or copies your client list into its storage.
