Confidentiality pre-check See it

Before text goes to an outside AI tool, Candor scans it for client identifying details: names, SSNs, account and policy numbers, dates, addresses, medical terms. It returns a redacted version to paste instead. Screening happens in the browser; the text itself is never stored. Try it below.

Verification See it

For research and drafting, Candor pulls out case citations and surfaces hedging language worth a second look, then checks each citation's existence against a public legal database. It's a prompt for the reviewer, not a verdict. Candor never claims a citation is correct.

Works with your case management

It files against the matters you already have.

AI use is filed against your real matters. Works with Clio, MyCase, Filevine, PracticePanther and Smokeball.

Your administrator authorises it

Sign in on the platform's own site, the standard way. Candor never sees a password, and your firm can cut off access from your side at any time without asking us.

Matters are read, never kept

Candor reads the matter list when it needs it and lets it go. Your client list is not copied into our storage, and every time it is read the access log records it.

Facts without the client name

Pull the matter number, practice area, and status straight into a prompt so nobody retypes them. The matter title stays out by default, because titles usually carry the client's name.

One thing to check on your side: private integrations need a mid tier plan or above at most platforms, and the person connecting has to be an administrator.

Governance around the work

The firm's AI policy, put into practice.

Your firm’s rules, applied at the moment someone uses AI and recorded with everything else. Candor does not decide what the rules should be.

Rules, pre-flight and review holds

Rules over the tool, the task, how sensitive the material is and who is doing it. Before a use is logged, Candor answers go ahead, review first, or not permitted, with the reasons.

Approved tools and vendor profiles

For each tool: whether it trains on what it is sent, how long it keeps it, whether a data agreement is on file, and a review date.

Risk radar

The last 30 days against the 30 before: flagged uses, unapproved tools, sensitive material, unsigned research and open incidents. Plain counts.

Matter files and receipts

Every AI use on a matter in order, with its sign-off and consent history. Any use prints as a one-page receipt.

Incidents

Record a problem when it is found, then each step to closed. Facts only, never edited afterwards.

Engagement-letter check

Candor points to the sentences about AI in a client’s engagement letter, read in your browser and never uploaded. The attorney decides what to record.

Short courses on using Candor and on your firm’s own policy, with quizzes. A rule can hold research until a course is passed.

Common questions

Questions firms ask about Candor

Does Candor store what I type into an AI tool?

No. The confidentiality screen runs in your browser, and Candor keeps only the fact that a check ran and which categories fired, never the text. Retaining any underlying content is an explicit choice a firm turns on, off by default.

Whose AI does Candor use, mine or its own?

Your firm's own model, on your own key. Every prompt is screened before it reaches the model, and Candor keeps the fact of each use, not its content.

Does Candor write anything into my case-management system?

Only if your firm turns it on. Candor reads the matter list when it needs it and never copies your client list into its storage. With Clio write-back switched on by an admin (it is off by default), each logged AI use becomes a note on its Clio matter, written under that person's own Clio login. The note records the fact of the use (tool, task, who, when), never a prompt or an answer.

Can Candor apply our firm's AI policy?

Yes, as rules your admin writes: which tools, which tasks, how sensitive the material, and for whom. Before a use is logged Candor gives your rules' answer (go ahead, needs review first, or not permitted) and the entry records it. Candor applies your rules; it does not decide what they should be, and it is not legal advice.

What does Candor record when something goes wrong?

The facts of an incident: what kind, how serious, the matter number, the tool, when it was found, and each step taken with its date. It keeps no written description, so the account of what happened stays in your firm's own files.

Does Candor read our engagement letters?

Only in your browser, and only when you pick one. Candor points to the sentences about AI so the attorney can record consent. Nothing from the letter is sent to Candor or kept.
