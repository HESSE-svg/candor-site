Pricing questions

Details

Any per use or token charges?

No. One predictable monthly (or annual) fee for the firm. Your AI tools bill you separately for their own usage; Candor doesn't meter on top of that.

Does it work inside ChatGPT and Claude?

Yes. An optional browser companion puts the screening, the citation check, and one click logging inside ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot, so your team does not have to open a separate app. It reads only the answer on the page, and sends out only the bare citations it needs to check.

What's the commitment?

Month to month is fine. Annual saves roughly two months. Design partners lock a founder rate for staying through the early period.

What do you think about the price?

We weigh it against the downside it addresses. A single malpractice question answered badly, or a filing gone wrong, dwarfs a year of subscription. Roughly an hour of attorney time a month buys a record your carrier will ask about.

Is there a free trial?

The design partner program is effectively that, with real support attached. Reach out and we'll set you up.
