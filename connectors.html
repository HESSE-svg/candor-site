What Candor works with.

Candor goes where your firm already does the work: the AI chat tools, the legal platforms, Clio and Word. Here is each one, and what Candor does there.

Inside the AI tools your firm already uses

A browser panel for screening a prompt, checking an answer’s citations and logging the use, without leaving the page.

  • ChatGPTPanel in the page
  • ClaudePanel in the page
  • GeminiPanel in the page
  • PerplexityPanel in the page
  • GrokPanel in the page
  • Meta AIPanel in the page
  • DeepSeekPanel in the page
  • Mistral Le ChatPanel in the page
  • PoePanel in the page
  • Microsoft CopilotHighlight or paste to check
  • Microsoft 365 CopilotHighlight or paste to check

On ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, Candor reads the AI’s answer only when someone clicks Check. On Microsoft sites it never reads the page; you highlight or paste the text you want checked.

Legal AI platforms

  • HarveyPanel; paste to check
  • Lexis+ AIPanel; paste to check
  • EvePanel; paste to check
  • CoCounselHighlight and right-click to check. No panel, in keeping with Thomson Reuters’ terms.
  • Any other AI toolHighlight or paste to check, including legal research tools

Practice management

  • ClioMatter permissions come from your Clio. If the firm turns it on, each logged use is written back to the matter as a note.

Documents

  • Microsoft WordAdd-in: screen and log inside the document

Browsers

  • ChromeSide panel and highlight-to-check
  • EdgeSide panel and highlight-to-check

Your firm’s own AI account
