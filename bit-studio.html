Company · Bit Studio

Bit Studio

Bit is a short animated tech-news series made by Candor Software LLC. Bit Studio is the internal tool that publishes each episode, once we have approved it, to Instagram and to our own YouTube channel.

Watch Bit on YouTubeBit Channel

How it works

  • We write and render each episode, and a person on our team reviews and approves it.
  • At the scheduled time, Bit Studio uploads that approved video once to Bit Channel through YouTube API Services, signed in with a candor.legal Google account.
  • It works only with our own channel. It is not offered to anyone outside Candor, and it has no public sign-in.

Voices

Bit’s voice is made with Kokoro (Apache 2.0). Pip’s voice is made with Pocket TTS by Kyutai (model under CC BY 4.0), using the “alba” voice recorded by Alba MacKenna (CC BY 4.0). Both are open-source models we run ourselves, so no script or audio goes to an outside voice service.

Terms of use

Bit Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using Bit Studio, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Candor’s own Terms also apply.

Privacy

What Bit Studio accesses, stores and deletes, and how to revoke its access, is in the Bit Studio section of our Privacy Policy. Google’s handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
