Security · Where data goes

What leaves, where it goes, and when.

Each outside service Candor or its browser extension sends anything to, with what is sent. This page covers the Candor software and its extension; the privacy page covers this website.

What stays in the browser

Text someone screens, highlights or pastes to check, AI answers read for a citation check, and documents picked for a source-consistency check are all handled in the browser and never uploaded. What leaves is the result: that a check ran, which kinds of details it flagged, the citation strings it found, and counts. The extension talks only to your firm’s own Candor account at app.candor.legal. If the firm turns on fingerprinting, fingerprints of an AI answer go to the firm’s own record; the answer itself does not.

Lookups for citation checks

CourtListener, run by Free Law Project

What is sent
Only the bare citation string, such as “410 U.S. 113”.
When
When a citation check runs: in the app, in the extension, or on the free citation check.
Never sent
The text around the citation, case names, client names or matter names.

Legal Information Institute (Cornell Law School) and govinfo (U.S. Government Publishing Office)

What is sent
A U.S. Code title and section number, such as “42” and “1983”.
When
When a statute is checked. govinfo is used only when it is turned on.
Never sent
Anything else.

Services your firm connects

Your firm’s own AI provider

What is sent
A prompt, after it has been screened, on the firm’s own key.
When
Only when someone uses Candor’s workspace (Ask). What the provider does with it is set by the firm’s own agreement with that provider.

Clio

What is sent
Requests for the matter list, under the firm’s connection or each person’s own login, to fill in matter numbers and to apply matter walls. If the firm turns on write-back (off by default), a note on the matching Clio matter recording the fact of an AI use: tool, task, who, when, the data label, whether the review statement was made, the billing flag and the Candor entry number.
When
Only once the firm connects Clio. The matter list is never stored; matter numbers are held in memory for up to five minutes.
Never sent
A prompt or an AI answer.

Services that run Candor

Render

What is sent
The firm’s record, which Render hosts with Candor’s database.
When
Always; this is where the Candor software runs, in the United States.

Clerk

What is sent
Each person’s name, email address and sign-in details, and the invitations an administrator sends.
When
When people sign in or are invited.

Stripe

What is sent
The plan, firm name, attorney count and email from checkout. Card details go only to Stripe.
When
At checkout and for billing.

Resend

What is sent
Candor’s service email: the welcome email, setup, trial and billing reminders, and messages sent from Help in the app.
When
When one of those emails goes out.

Google Workspace

What is sent
Candor’s own inbox, where Help and Contact messages arrive. Each message is checked for client details first and refused if any are found.
When
When someone writes to Candor.

The companies that process a firm’s personal data on Candor’s behalf are the approved subprocessors in Annex III of the DPA.
