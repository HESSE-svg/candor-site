## Workflow

### Step 1: Fill in prospect lists
- Use the CSV files as your source of truth
- Add emails as you find them (LinkedIn, company websites, Twitter bios)
- Mark status: `pending` (email not found), `ready` (email confirmed), `sent` (outreach done)

### Step 2: Broker outreach (1-2 hours for 50 emails)
1. Batch send to 5-10 brokers at a time (avoid bulk email filters)
2. Use Gmail BCC (put your email in BCC for tracking)
3. Send on Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-2pm (best open rates)
4. Update CSV column `Status` to `sent`

### Step 3: Newsletter outreach (ongoing)
1. Pick 2-3 publications per week
2. Personalize each pitch (editor name, why it matters for their readers)
3. Track in CSV or separate spreadsheet
4. Expect 10-20% response rate (links in legal tech press = domain authority)

### Step 4: Monitor & follow up
- Broker: Follow up at 5-7 days if no response
- Newsletter: Assume silence = no interest; move on
- Track responses in the CSV or a separate sheet

## Tips
- **Personalization matters**: Add a line about why YOU think they're a good fit (their recent posts, their firm's focus)
- **Don't spam**: Send in batches of 5-10, not 100 at once (Gmail flags as spam)
- **Timing**: Avoid Mondays (too busy), avoid Fridays (goes in the pile)
- **Follow-up is gold**: A second email 7 days later doubles response rate
- **Track everything**: Even "no thanks" responses tell you who's aware of Candor
