What Candor is

One place to use AI, and the record of how you used it.

Candor is AI governance software for law firms. Staff draft and research on the firm’s own AI account, in Candor or in the tools they already open. Client details are swapped out before a prompt is sent, citations that may need a second look are flagged, and each use is filed to its matter with the attorney’s sign-off, in a record no one can quietly edit. When a carrier or a court asks how the firm uses AI, the answer is already written down.
