Why now

The gap is about to become a requirement.

Most firms adopted AI faster than they adopted any rules about it — no log of what was run, no record of who reviewed it, no way to show anyone the work was supervised. Three forces are closing in at once.

Carriers

Malpractice insurers are beginning to ask about AI use at renewal. Firms that can document their controls are in a very different position than firms that can't.

Regulators

Bar associations are issuing AI guidance on competence, confidentiality, and supervision. The expectations are becoming concrete.

Courts

Judges are issuing standing orders on AI disclosure and certification. Sanctions for fabricated citations are already on the books.

How it works

One connected record — from first prompt to final report.

Six steps, built to sit inside how a firm already works. No browser extensions, no traffic interception, nothing that slows anyone down.

01

Confidentiality pre-check

Before anyone pastes text into an outside AI tool, Candor scans it for client-identifying details — names, SSNs, account and policy numbers, dates, medical terms — and hands back a redacted version. The text is screened in the browser and never stored.

02

AI use log

A per-matter record of every AI use: the tool, the task, who ran it, and when — measured against the firm's approved-tools list, so anything off-list is flagged.

03

Verification

On research and drafting, Candor extracts case citations and flags hedging language for a second look, checking each citation's existence against a public legal database. An assist for the reviewer — never a guarantee.

04

Attorney review & sign-off

A supervising attorney reviews the work and signs off, attesting they independently verified the citations and assertions. Sign-offs are append-only — corrections are new entries, never edits.

05

Reports

A firm-wide compliance summary and a carrier-renewal questionnaire, generated straight from the record — the artifact you actually hand across the table.

06

Policy management

Adopt a written AI-use policy; staff acknowledge it with a timestamp and re-acknowledge whenever it changes.

Built for the record

Trustworthy by construction.

A record is only worth as much as its integrity. Candor is built so the record holds up.

Confidential by default

Screening runs in the browser. The text you check is never uploaded, logged, or stored — Candor keeps the fact that a check ran and which categories fired, never the content.

Append-only

Logs and sign-offs are never edited. A correction is a new entry that references the one before it, and the full chain is preserved. That immutability is what makes the record credible to a third party.

Per-firm isolation

Every record is scoped to the firm. No cross-tenant access, by design.

Honest about limits

Candor does not give legal advice, determine compliance, or guarantee any AI output. It produces the record your attorneys use to make those judgments themselves.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this a compliance product?

Candor is a record-keeping tool, not a compliance authority. It doesn't decide whether your firm is compliant with any rule — it produces the documentation your attorneys use to demonstrate how AI is supervised at the firm. That distinction is deliberate.

Do you store the confidential text we screen?

No. The confidentiality pre-check runs entirely in your browser. Candor records that a check happened and which categories of sensitive data were found — never the underlying text.

Does Candor verify that AI output is accurate?

No — and we're careful never to claim otherwise. The verification step flags citations and uncertain language that deserve a second look and confirms whether a cited case exists. The attorney's independent review and sign-off carry the legal weight, not Candor's output.

Will this slow my team down?

It's built to sit inside how people already work — paste-and-screen, a quick per-matter log entry, a one-click sign-off. No browser extensions, no traffic interception, no new system to babysit.

How does it help at insurance renewal?

Carriers are starting to ask how firms use AI. Candor produces a renewal-ready summary mapped to those questions and answered from your actual record — so you're documenting controls, not scrambling to describe them.

What does it cost?
