Pipeline Definitions
Broker stages:
Prospect No contact yet
Ready to send Email prepared, waiting to send
Email sent Outreach sent, awaiting response
Responded They replied to your email
Demo scheduled Meeting booked
Closed / Won They committed or passed

Newsletter stages:
Prospect Not contacted yet
Pitch sent Tip or pitch sent
Responded Editor replied
Published Article, mention, or backlink secured
How to use this dashboard:
• Track every broker and publication contact
• Move contacts through stages as they progress (prospect → demo → closed)
• Update "Last Action" date each time you touch a prospect
• Export CSV whenever you want a backup
• Refresh the page to see latest counts

Broker demo prep: When moving to "demo scheduled", prepare: product walkthrough (5 min), their clients' pain points (2 min), pricing/terms (1 min), next steps (2 min). Total: 15 min = buffer for questions.
