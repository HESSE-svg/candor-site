What that record proves

Not that the AI was right, but that a lawyer took responsibility.

The flag is a prompt, not a verdict

Candor surfaces citations that may need a second look and the AI's own hedging. It doesn't verify accuracy or decide compliance; the attorney's sign-off carries the weight, and confirming the citation is still their job.

Every decision is dated and attributed

Who reviewed it, when, what they decided, and any note, kept as an append-only entry. A reversal references the original; nothing is overwritten.

This is the artifact for the carrier

When a malpractice carrier asks how AI is used at the firm, this loop (logged use, a flag, an attorney sign-off) is the record you hand them.
