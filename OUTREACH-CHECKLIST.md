## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't send >20 emails in one day (Gmail flags as spam)
- ❌ Don't use generic greetings ("Hi there")
- ❌ Don't wait for perfection (send now, iterate)
- ❌ Don't give up on brokers after first contact (follow-up doubles response)
- ❌ Don't attach full documents (link instead, reduce email size)
