Changelog

What shipped, and when.

Candor is pre-launch and built close to the ground. This is the dated record of what has actually shipped, the same honesty the product is about.

Updates

2026-10-01 · More panels, agent runs and sharing with underwriters

The browser panel now opens on Eve, Harvey, CoCounsel and Lexis+ AI. A run by an AI agent is logged as one use, with its steps. The carrier renewal questionnaire can be shared with an underwriter through a frozen link that expires and can be revoked. A firm can turn on a public AI trust page and link to it from its own website. Citation results now say whether a case was found or needs a person to review it.

2026-09-27 · Governance around the work

The firm's own AI-use rules with a pre-flight answer before each use (in the app and the browser panel), approved tools with vendor profiles and review dates, a risk radar for the managing partner, a file for each matter and a one-page receipt for each use, facts-only incident records, an engagement-letter consent check that runs in the browser, and Candor Academy training that rules can require. The app menu is reorganized around when you need each part.

2026-09-14 · Guided first-run setup

A new firm now lands on a setup checklist (connect your model, adopt a policy, name approved tools, invite your team, pair a browser, run a first confidentiality check) instead of an empty screen.

2026-09-13 · Automatic firm setup from signup

A completed signup can now provision the firm automatically, creating the workspace and setting the plan, rather than waiting on a manual step.

2026-09-12 · One toolkit, mapped across professions

The governance toolkit is now industry-aware under the hood, so the same record can extend beyond law firms over time. See Our plan.

2026-09-11 · Time Saved, plans & seats, live checkout

An honest hours-saved view (attorney-supplied estimates minus measured AI time), attorney-seat plans, and a self-serve one-month-free checkout.

2026-08-23 · Firm AI Risk Assessment

An aggregate-only, no-surveillance measure of a practice group's AI exposure; attestation available on every task type, not only research and drafting.

2026-08-22 · Browser extension and Word add-in

Screen, check citations, and log AI use at the point of use (inside ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and Word) with capture only on an explicit action, never by watching a session.

Earlier · The core record

Confidentiality pre-check, the per-matter AI use log, citation verification against CourtListener, attorney sign-off, deterministic carrier reports, and the append-only, hash-chained, encrypted record underneath it all.

Dates are when the work shipped to the product, not marketing announcements.
