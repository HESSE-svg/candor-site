# Candor copy rules

Candor is software that keeps a law firm's record of AI use. The words must
never promise more than the software does. Run every draft through this list.

## Never say
- That Candor **verifies**, **confirms** or **guarantees** accuracy or
  citations. Say it "flags citations that may need a second look."
- That Candor makes a firm **compliant** or "keeps you compliant." Say it
  keeps "the record your firm uses to show how it used AI."
- Anything that sounds like **legal advice**, or that Candor is a **law firm**.
- That **Jesse is an attorney**. He is a former claims adjuster.
- **"Candor Legal"**. An unrelated firm uses that name. The brand is "Candor".
- Anything about **fee sharing**. Candor is a flat-fee software subscription.
- Invented quotes, customers, press or numbers. Every figure needs a source.

## Always
- Reduce risk, don't eliminate it: "helps", "flags", "records", "shows".
- Vary the wording. If a phrase ("the record that proves…") carried one
  section, say the next idea differently. No repeated sentence shapes.
- Fictional examples use obviously invented names (Jane Roe) and say so.
- No third-party slogans or trademarks (no "Priceless", no "stays in Vegas").
  Public phrases like the Miranda warning are fine.
- Product names of AI tools come with "names are trademarks of their owners"
  when listed together.

## Facts worth reusing (checked)
- The largest U.S. AI-citation sanction on record: $110,204, Couvrette v.
  Wisnovsky (D. Or. 2026), sanctions plus the other side's fees, split between
  two lawyers. Link /sanctions/couvrette-v-wisnovsky.
- Pricing: flat annual fee by attorney count, $3,000–$20,000; staff free;
  one month free; runs on the firm's own AI account (no AI markup).
- Tagline: "Proof your firm did the work."

## Brand look (web and reports)
Navy #1B2A41, paper #FAF8F5, ink #22201D, oxblood #7A2E2E (accent and
buttons only), seal green #2F5D50 (success), brass #9A6B1F (warnings, never
bright red). Serif headlines, Inter for body. No gradients, no drop shadows,
no stock gavels or scales.
