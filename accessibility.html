Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want candor.legal and the Candor app to work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard instead of a mouse, magnification, or reduced motion.

Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

How we check

  • Every page of this site was last tested on September 29, 2026 with axe-core, the open-source accessibility engine, at desktop and phone sizes, with no violations found.
  • We also check by hand: every page can be used with a keyboard alone, shows where the keyboard focus is, reads correctly at 400% zoom without sideways scrolling, and respects your device’s reduced-motion setting.
  • Anything that moves on its own can be paused.

Known limits

  • The product walkthrough is an animation with on-screen text and no sound. It can be paused, replayed and slowed down, and the same information is written out on the product page.
  • Automated tools cannot catch everything, and we are a small team. If something here or in the app gets in your way, we want to know.

Tell us about a barrier

Email jesse@candor.legal with the page and what happened. A person will reply, usually within two business days, and we will help you get what you need in another way while we fix it.
