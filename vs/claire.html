Product · Candor vs Claire

An AI autopilot for many industries, or a record built only for law firms?

Claire covers AI compliance for small businesses in several industries, law among them. Candor does one thing: keep a law firm's record of how its people use AI, from the moment something is pasted to the attorney's sign-off. Here is how the two line up, based on Claire's public pages as of September 2026.

FeatureClaireCandor
Who it's built forSmall and mid-size businesses across healthcare, legal, finance and hospitalityU.S. law firms, and nothing else
How it describes itselfAn AI compliance “autopilot” and digital-labor platformThe record of how a firm uses AI, kept where the work happens
AI policyA governance policy template for small firmsThe firm's own rules, applied before each use, with staff acknowledgments on the record
Attorney reviewReview gates that record which attorney reviewed the work, per its legal pagesA sign-off queue for every logged use; firm rules can mark work “needs review first”; the attorney's verification statement is recorded
BillingTracks time with AI against the time the task would have taken without itTime-saved reports, and a prompt to disclose to the client when AI use affects the bill
Inside ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and CopilotNot described in the public pages we foundA browser panel on the AI tools staff already use: screen, check and log without switching apps
Screening client details before they're sentNot described in the public pages we foundCatches names, Social Security, policy and account numbers, dates of birth and more, in the browser, before the paste
The recordReview records and documentationAppend-only and hash-chained, so any change shows; exportable at any time
PricingNot publishedPublished: $3,000–$20,000 a year by attorney count, staff free, one month free

This compares published focus, not quality. Sources: claire.legal and Claire's small-firm legal page, read September 2026. If your firm wants one vendor for several kinds of business, Claire is built for that. If you want the record a malpractice carrier can read, that's the Candor side.

Why Candor for law firms

A template says what you meant to do. A record shows what happened.

Policies and review gates matter, and Candor has both. The difference is where the record comes from: Candor builds it while staff work, inside the AI tools they already use, and seals each entry to the one before it. When a carrier or a court asks, the firm shows the file, not a promise.
