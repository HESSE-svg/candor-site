Product · Candor vs Claire

An AI autopilot for many industries, or a record built only for law firms?

Claire covers AI compliance for small businesses in several industries, law among them. Candor does one thing: keep a law firm's record of how its people use AI, from the moment something is pasted to the attorney's sign-off. Here is how the two line up, based on Claire's public pages as of September 2026.

Why Candor for law firms

A template says what you meant to do. A record shows what happened.

Policies and review gates matter, and Candor has both. The difference is where the record comes from: Candor builds it while staff work, inside the AI tools they already use, and seals each entry to the one before it. When a carrier or a court asks, the firm shows the file, not a promise.
