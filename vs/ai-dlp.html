Product · Candor vs AI data-loss tools

Blocking a leak is one job. Proving how the firm used AI is another.

Data-loss prevention tools for AI, from vendors such as Nightfall, Harmonic Security and Microsoft Purview, watch what people type into ChatGPT and stop sensitive data leaving. They're built for companies with a security team. Candor catches client details too, and then does the part a law firm is actually asked about: the matter log, the attorney's sign-off, and the record.

FeatureAI data-loss toolsCandor
Who it's built forCompanies with an IT or security teamLaw firms without an IT department
Catching sensitive data before it reaches an AI toolYes: broad detection of personal, health, payment and secret data, often with blockingYes: client names, Social Security, policy and account numbers, dates of birth, medical terms and dollar figures, flagged in the browser with a redacted copy
How it's installedUsually managed devices, endpoint agents or a managed browser, set up by ITSign up online; staff pair the browser panel in one click, or IT installs it for everyone
Matter-by-matter log of AI useNot its jobEvery use filed to its matter: tool, task, who, when
Attorney sign-offNot its jobA sign-off queue, with the attorney's verification statement recorded
Citations that may need a second lookNot its jobFlags cited cases that may need a second look (CourtListener)
A report for the malpractice carrierNot its jobA renewal questionnaire and firm summary built from the record
PricingVaries by vendor; mostly sold to enterprise security teamsPublished: $3,000–$20,000 a year by attorney count, staff free

This compares the general category, not any one product; each vendor's features differ. If you have a security team and need company-wide data controls, an AI data-loss tool fits, and it can run alongside Candor. If you need to show a carrier or a court how your firm used AI on client matters, that's Candor.

Why Candor for law firms

“Blocked” isn't an answer a carrier can use.

A carrier's renewal questions are about process: which tools, on which matters, who reviewed the output, and whether citations were checked. A blocked paste leaves no answer to any of them. Candor screens first, then keeps the answers as the work happens.
