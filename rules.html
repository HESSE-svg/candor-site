AI rules for lawyers, by state

Which AI-related professional duties apply to your firm, and where they come from. Each summary is tied to a specific bar opinion or rule, so nothing here is asserted without an authority behind it. A reference summary of published guidance, not legal advice.

Rules by state

Florida

The four duties under Florida Bar Ethics Opinion 24-1: consent, billing, verification, and court-filing disclosure.

National baseline (ABA)

ABA Formal Opinion 512, the reference point most firms start from where no state opinion is on point.

Interactive tool

Pick a jurisdiction and compare the duties side by side, each with its source link.
Honest about coverage

We add a state only when a real opinion backs it.

Florida and the national ABA baseline are covered today. More states are added one at a time, each drawn from its own bar opinion, statute, or court rule with the source linked, never by relabeling a generic template. If your state has not issued specific AI guidance yet, most firms follow ABA Formal Opinion 512 as the starting point.
